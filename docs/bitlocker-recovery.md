# BitLocker Recovery Key

Applies to: company Windows laptops. Owner: Endpoint team.

## Why you see the BitLocker recovery screen
BitLocker asks for a 48-digit recovery key after BIOS updates, hardware changes, or several wrong PIN attempts.

## Get your recovery key
1. On another device, go to https://myaccount.microsoft.com/device-list and sign in.
2. Select your laptop, then "View BitLocker Keys".
3. Match the Key ID shown on the laptop screen and type the 48-digit key.
If you cannot access another device, call the Service Desk at extension 4357. Have the laptop's asset tag ready (on the sticker on the bottom of the laptop). The Service Desk reads the key to you after verifying your identity.

## Notes
- Never write the recovery key on the laptop or store it in a file on the laptop.
- If the recovery screen appears again after every restart, open a ticket so the Endpoint team can check the TPM.
