# Add a Printer and Fix Print Problems

Applies to: office printers. Owner: Service Desk.

## Add a printer
All office printers use secure print (follow-me printing).
- Windows: open Settings > Bluetooth & devices > Printers & scanners > Add device, and choose "SecurePrint-Queue".
- Mac: open System Settings > Printers & Scanners > Add Printer, and choose "SecurePrint-Queue".
Then badge in at any printer to release your job. Jobs that are not released are deleted after 24 hours.

## Common problems
- Job does not appear at the printer: make sure you printed to SecurePrint-Queue, not a direct printer.
- Badge not recognized: badges must be registered once. Tap the badge, then sign in at the printer with your username and the PIN from the badge registration email.
- Paper jam or toner: follow the instructions on the printer screen. Facilities replaces toner; email facilities@example.org.

Open a ticket if a printer shows an error code for more than 30 minutes.
