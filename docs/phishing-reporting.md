# Report Phishing and Suspicious Activity

Applies to: all staff. Owner: Security team.

## Report a suspicious email
In Outlook, select the email and click the "Report" button, then choose "Report phishing". This sends it to the Security team and removes it from your inbox. Do not forward suspicious emails to coworkers.

## If you clicked a link or entered your password
1. Disconnect from VPN and Wi-Fi.
2. Call the Security hotline at extension 7700 immediately, 24/7.
3. Change your password from a different device.
Clicking a link is not a disciplinary issue; reporting it quickly is what matters.

## Unexpected MFA prompts
If you receive MFA prompts you did not start, deny them and call extension 7700. Repeated prompts can mean someone has your password.

## Never
- Never share your password or MFA codes, including with IT staff. IT will never ask for them.
- Never disable or uninstall CrowdStrike Falcon or other security tools.
