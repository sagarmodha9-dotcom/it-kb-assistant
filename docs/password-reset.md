# Reset or Change Your Password

Applies to: all network accounts. Owner: Identity team.

## Password rules
- At least 14 characters. Passphrases are encouraged.
- Cannot reuse any of your last 10 passwords.
- Passwords expire every 365 days. You get email reminders starting 14 days before expiry.

## Forgot your password (self-service)
1. Go to https://passwordreset.example.org from any browser.
2. Enter your work email and complete MFA.
3. Choose a new password that meets the rules above.
Self-service reset works only if you registered MFA first.

## Account locked
Accounts lock after 10 failed sign-in attempts and unlock automatically after 15 minutes. If you need access sooner, call the Service Desk at extension 4357.

## After changing your password
- Sign out and back in on your laptop while connected to the office network or VPN, so the cached password updates.
- Update the password on your phone's Outlook and Teams apps.
- Wi-Fi on your phone may prompt for the new password on "Example-Corp".
