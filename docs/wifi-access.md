# Wi-Fi Networks in the Office

Applies to: all offices. Owner: Network team.

## Networks
- Example-Corp: for company-managed laptops and phones. Connects automatically with your work account. Do not connect personal devices.
- Example-Guest: for visitors and personal devices. Internet only, no access to internal systems. The weekly guest password is posted at the front desk and changes every Monday.

## Troubleshooting Example-Corp
1. Turn Wi-Fi off and on.
2. "Forget" Example-Corp and reconnect with your work email and current password.
3. If you recently changed your password, sign out and back in on the laptop.
4. If the laptop still cannot connect, plug into a wired port and open a ticket.

## Visitors
Visitors use Example-Guest only. Visitors who need internal access must be sponsored by a manager through a software and access request.
