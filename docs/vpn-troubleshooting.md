# VPN: Connect and Troubleshoot

Applies to: remote and hybrid staff. Owner: Network team.

## Connecting
The VPN client is GlobalProtect. Open GlobalProtect from the system tray (Windows) or menu bar (Mac), enter the portal address vpn.example.org, and sign in with your work account and MFA.

## Common problems
- "Gateway not responding": switch networks (for example from hotel Wi-Fi to a phone hotspot). Many public networks block VPN traffic.
- Connected but can't reach internal sites: disconnect, wait 10 seconds, reconnect. If it continues, restart the laptop.
- Repeated MFA prompts: make sure the time on your laptop is set automatically.
- Slow over VPN: video calls in Teams do not need the VPN; disconnect from VPN during long video meetings.

## When to contact the Service Desk
Open a ticket if GlobalProtect shows "Portal not found" after a restart, or if more than one coworker reports the same VPN problem at the same time (that may be an outage). Include your location, network type, and a screenshot of the error.
